Reflection

How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?
I interpret user needs by looking at what the customer wants the program to accomplish and breaking those needs into smaller requirements. User stories help because they explain a feature from the user's point of view and give the development team a clear goal. They also make larger requirements easier to organize, prioritize, develop, and test.

How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
I approach developing programs by breaking the work into smaller parts instead of trying to complete everything at once. I would like to continue using Agile practices such as user stories, Sprint Planning, regular testing, feedback, and retrospectives. These practices can help keep work organized while still allowing changes when requirements or customer needs change.

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
I think being a good team member means communicating clearly, completing your responsibilities, asking questions when needed, and being willing to help others. Software development depends on people with different roles working together, so sharing information and listening to feedback are important. A good team member should also be flexible and willing to make improvements when problems are identified.
